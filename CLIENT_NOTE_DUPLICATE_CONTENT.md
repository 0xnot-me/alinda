# Note on the “Duplicate Content” Report

**For:** Linda R. Olsson, Inc.  
**Re:** Siteliner / duplicate content findings on lindaolsson.com

---

## Short version

The duplicate content report is **not a problem for your site** and does **not** mean we need to change your page structure or rewrite the website.

---

## Why that report looks the way it does

Your current website was rebuilt to **keep the same page addresses (URLs)** as the previous WordPress sites. That was intentional.

Those older sites had been live for years. People, other websites, Google, emails, and print materials still point to those links. Keeping the same structure protects:

- Search rankings you’ve already earned  
- Old bookmarks and shared links  
- Continuity for clients who return to a familiar page  

When a tool like Siteliner scans the site, it often labels a lot of content as “duplicate” or “common.” On a real estate website, that usually means things that **appear on every page on purpose**, such as:

- The navigation menu  
- The footer and contact details  
- Repeated branding language about Palm Beach luxury real estate  

That is normal. It is not the same as copying another company’s website or publishing thin, spammy pages.

---

## What this means for SEO

Search engines expect professional sites to share a header, footer, and core messaging. That alone does **not** hurt rankings in a meaningful way.

What *would* hurt SEO is renaming or removing the old WordPress-style pages just to make a “duplicate content” score look better. That could break historical links and weaken the authority those URLs already have.

---

## Bottom line

| Report finding | Our recommendation |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Duplicate / common content across pages | **Leave as is** — expected for this site |
| Same URL structure as the old WordPress sites | **Keep** — protects rankings and old links |
| Site redesign / page restructuring because of this report | **Not needed** |

We will continue to improve performance, security, and content quality where it actually helps. The duplicate content report does not require structural changes.
